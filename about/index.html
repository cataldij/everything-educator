About

Twenty years in the room.

I am Jon Cataldi. I teach Spanish and world language, and I have been doing it for twenty years. The Everything Educator is where the material I build for my own students ends up once it is clean enough for somebody else to print.

Why the files look like this

Before I went back to full time teaching I was Director of AI and Technology for an international K to 12 school network. Part of that job was watching good material fail in practice, usually for boring reasons. The font was not installed. The name was wrong and could not be fixed. The color version ate a cartridge and there was no black and white version. The file assumed a device every kid had, right up until the wifi went down.

So the rules here are simple and they do not change. Everything is editable in PowerPoint and Google Slides. Everything prints in color and in black and white. Nothing needs a font you have to install. Nothing needs a device.

The Spanish side

A good part of the store is written in Spanish, for Spanish class, by the person teaching it. That matters more than it sounds. Most Spanish classroom decor is English decor run through a translator, and students notice the difference between language that was written and language that was converted.

What you can expect

  • Real page previews. Every listing shows what actually prints.
  • Editable text, every time, so the names and the wording end up being yours.
  • Color and an ink saving black and white version in the same file.
  • A straight answer if you email me about a file. I read it.
