Recursos Gratis en Español

What it is

Spanish-language classroom printables: a welcome banner, editable labels for classroom materials, and four routine posters. The labels and posters come pre-filled and as a blank copy you can retype.

Best for
Kindergarten through grade 3, Spanish-language classrooms
Prep
Print at actual size on cardstock and laminate whatever you want to last.
Grades
Kindergarten, 1st grade, 2nd grade, 3rd grade
Pages
16
Type
Printables

Download free on TPT

It opens on Teachers Pay Teachers, where the file lives. A free TPT account is needed to download.

Ways to use it

  1. Hang the welcome banner across the door or a bulletin board before the first day, so students see Spanish as soon as they walk in.
  2. Label bins, baskets and supplies with the material labels, and retype any word on the blank copy to match your own setup.
  3. Post the four routine posters where each routine happens and point students to them during transitions.
