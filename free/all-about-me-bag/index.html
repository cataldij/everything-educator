All About Me Bag

What it is

A 9-page editable set for an all about me bag project, where each child fills a bag with five objects that say something about them and presents them to the class. It comes with a family note, bag toppers, a packing planner, prompt cards and a listening sheet.

Best for
Preschool, kindergarten and grade 1
Prep
Edit the family note in PowerPoint or Google Slides, print at actual size, and have a paper bag ready for each child.
Grades
Preschool, Kindergarten, 1st Grade
Pages
9
Type
Printables

Download free on TPT

It opens on Teachers Pay Teachers, where the file lives. A free TPT account is needed to download.

Ways to use it

  1. Send the family note and a bag home in the first weeks of school so children have a few days to choose their five things.
  2. Have each child complete the My 5 Things to Pack page before packing, so every item has a reason behind it.
  3. Spread share day over several mornings, using the prompt cards for the presenter and the listening sheet to give the audience a job.
