What is in the file

A free all about me bag activity for the first weeks of school: a child takes home a kraft bag, fills it with 5 things that show who they are, and shares it with the class. This set goes further than a note and a topper. It includes an editable parent note home explaining the activity, bag toppers in both color and black and white, a planning sheet the child fills in before packing so the five things are chosen with a reason, share day prompt cards that cue exactly what to say while presenting, an audience listening sheet so classmates have a job while they watch, and a teacher how-to page. Editable in PowerPoint and Google Slides.

Made for preschool, kindergarten and 1st grade. You get 9 pages, as an editable PowerPoint and a print-ready PDF.
