Realidades 1 Capitulo 1A FREE Study Guide

What it is

A first-year Spanish study unit for Capitulo 1A, the free-time chapter. It has a vocabulary bank of more than 35 free-time activities, a grammar focus on me gusta plus an infinitive, a short Novice reading with five questions, and a short writing task.

Ways to use it

  1. Hand out the vocabulary bank at the start of the chapter so students can see the free-time words grouped by type.
  2. Teach me gusta plus an infinitive from the worked models, then have students try the eight guided practice items and check them against the answer key.
  3. Assign the reading, its five questions and the writing task as independent practice or sub work, with students typing their answers into the editable PowerPoint.
