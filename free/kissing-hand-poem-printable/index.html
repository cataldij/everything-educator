FREE Kissing Hand Poem Printable

What it is

A first-day-of-school poem keepsake with four parts: the poem, a handprint keepsake page, a My Kiss in My Hand page and a When I Miss Home page. It goes with the day-one read-aloud and does not include the book.

Best for
Pre-K through grade 1
Prep
Print the pages and have washable paint ready for the handprints.
Grades
Pre-K, Kindergarten, 1st grade
Pages
10
Type
Printables

Download free on TPT

It opens on Teachers Pay Teachers, where the file lives. A free TPT account is needed to download.

Ways to use it

  1. After the day-one read-aloud, have each child add a painted handprint beside the poem.
  2. Send the finished page home so families have a keepsake from the first day.
  3. Offer the When I Miss Home page to a child who is having a hard time at drop-off or later in the morning.
