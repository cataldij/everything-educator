FREE Jeopardy Game

What it is

A Jeopardy-style review game in PowerPoint and Google Slides with an under-the-sea theme. You type in six categories of five questions plus a Final Round, and teams pick from a clickable board.

Best for
Grades 1 to 12, any subject
Prep
Replace the placeholder categories and questions with your own, then press F5 to present.

Download free on TPT

It opens on Teachers Pay Teachers, where the file lives. A free TPT account is needed to download.

Ways to use it

  1. Fill the board with questions from your math, reading, science or social studies unit and use it as a test review.
  2. Play it with two to four teams, with a Daily Double and a Final Round built in to add a little suspense at the end.
  3. Pair it with an ocean or sea life unit, writing the categories around what your class has been studying.
