World Cup 2026 FREE Fun Facts & Geography

What it is

A six-page soccer nonfiction pack with fact pages, a page on the geography of the three 2026 host nations (the USA, Canada and Mexico), and a lined writing page where students act as a sports reporter.

Ways to use it

  1. Read the fact pages with students as a nonfiction text, then ask what surprised them.
  2. Put a wall map or atlas beside the geography page so students can find the three host nations.
  3. Assign the reporter page as a writing task after reading, with students using the facts they just learned.
