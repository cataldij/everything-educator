What is in the file

This is a free sample of Feelings Thermometer.

Inside you get a free feelings chart: a feelings thermometer poster with five levels from Calm to Too Big, each with a number, a word, an illustrated face and a color. You also get a feelings check-in page students fill in after a hard moment, and 10 illustrated coping choice cards to cut apart: take 3 slow breaths, get a drink of water, squeeze a stress ball, count to 10, stretch up high, hug a stuffed animal, draw or color, ask an adult for help, listen to quiet music and read a book. These are the same pages as in the paid kit, so you can see exactly what they look like before you buy. The paid Feelings Thermometer adds five level posters, desk thermometers, blank coping cards, a calm-down plan, a make-your-own thermometer, a weekly log, a teacher data sheet and 10 How Big Is the Feeling? cards. It is a classroom visual support, not a diagnosis or a treatment.

Made for kindergarten through fifth grade. You get 5 pages, as an editable PowerPoint and a print-ready PDF.
