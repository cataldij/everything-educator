What it is

A set of multiplication charts: a finished times table chart from 1 to 12, blank 12 by 12 and 10 by 10 grids, two partly filled practice charts, and a patterns page on multiples of 2, 5 and 10.

Ways to use it

  1. Hand out a blank chart as a fact fluency challenge, then let students check their work against the filled chart.
  2. Use the partly filled charts as a quick warm-up where students complete the missing facts.
  3. Use the patterns page to have students study multiples of 2, 5 and 10 and say what they notice.
