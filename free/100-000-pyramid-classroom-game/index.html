HOW IT PLAYS

  1. Split into 2 teams. Each round one teammate gives clues, partner guesses.
  2. Round 1: 6 categories, each with 7 words. 30 seconds per category.
  3. Clue-giver describes each word, partner guesses. CANNOT say the target word!
  4. Got it right? Click GOT IT to advance to next word.
  5. Stuck? Click SKIP (-1 point penalty, advance).
  6. Round 2: 6 harder categories, teams switch clue-giver role.
  7. WINNER'S CIRCLE bonus: 60 seconds, 6 categories ascending $1K → $100K.
  8. Highest total wins. Vocabulary review with REAL active recall.
