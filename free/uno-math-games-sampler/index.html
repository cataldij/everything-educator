What is in the file

A completely free 28-card sampler of Math Monsters, a UNO-style card game where every colored card shows a math fact instead of a number, so students match by ANSWER or by COLOR. Every single turn is a solved fact, and they never notice they are drilling.

  • 28 print-and-play cards, two monster suits, numbers 0-9, plus Skip, Reverse, Draw Two and Wild cards. Enough for a real two-player game, not just a preview.
  • Addition within 10, great for Kindergarten through 2nd grade.
  • Full illustrated card backs so the deck looks like a real game once it is cut.
  • Rules page included, print, cut, play. No prep beyond cardstock.

Perfect for math centers, early finishers, indoor recess, small-group intervention, and sub tubs. Print on cardstock and laminate once and it lasts all year.
