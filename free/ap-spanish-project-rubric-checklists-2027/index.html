Free AP Spanish Project Rubric Checklists 2027

What it is

Two student self-check lists written in Spanish for the AP Spanish course project, one for the presentation and one for the Q&A, plus a rehearsal log and a calendar. They put the scoring rows in plain words and are not the official scoring guidelines.

Ways to use it

  1. After each practice presentation, have students tick every line of the presentation checklist and log their points by row.
  2. Pair students for peer review: a partner points to the line that needs work without rewriting anything or supplying wording.
  3. Use the rehearsal log and the calendar to keep a class on pace from the course project packet to exam day.
