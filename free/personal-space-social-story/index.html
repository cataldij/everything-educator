What is in the file

A free personal space social story for Pre-K to 3rd grade, with 11 illustrated pages. A hula hoop shows the space bubble around your body, then the story walks through leaving a little space in line, keeping your hands in your own space on the rug, taking a step back when you get too close, standing one arm away to talk, and asking before a hug. You also get a Personal Space Rules poster with six picture steps and a home note for families. One picture and one or two short sentences on every page, at a first and second grade reading level.
