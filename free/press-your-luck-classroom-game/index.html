What is in the file

PRESS YOUR LUCK CLASSROOM. EDITABLE 18-SQUARE REVIEW GAME. NO WHAMMIES!. Editable 80s-retro Press Your Luck review game. 18 squares: money, multipliers, and Whammies. Spin the board, answer review questions, NO WHAMMIES!

HOW IT PLAYS

  1. Split the class into 3 teams. Each team takes turns spinning.
  2. Click SPIN! → animation lands on a random square.
  3. Click the landed square → review question appears.
  4. CORRECT answer = claim that square's prize (money OR 2x/3x multiplier).
  5. WRONG answer = automatic WHAMMY (Whammy steals 25% of team's points).
  6. After correct: choose PASS (bank winnings) or PLAY (spin again, risk for more).
  7. WHAMMY squares hit automatically, no question, just steal!
  8. Highest total after all 18 squares revealed wins the game.
