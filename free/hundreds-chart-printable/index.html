Free Hundreds Chart Printable

What it is

A set of hundreds charts for kindergarten through grade 2: 1 to 100, 0 to 99 and 1 to 120 charts, blank 100 and 120 charts, two missing numbers pages, and a page of five ways to use a chart.

Ways to use it

  1. Slide a laminated chart into each student's math folder for counting practice and pattern hunts.
  2. Use the two missing numbers pages as a station or early finisher task: one hides a quarter of the numbers and the other hides half.
  3. Hand out a blank chart for a fill-in challenge, or retype the numbers in the editable file to make a version for your own class.
