FREE Behavior Tracker Editable

What it is

An editable behavior tracker for kindergarten through grade 3: a daily points sheet scored by time block on Safe, Respectful and Ready, and an on-task interval data sheet. Each page comes filled in as an example and blank, with a setup checklist.

Time
About 15 minutes to set up
Best for
Kindergarten through grade 3, including special education settings
Prep
Edit the expectations in PowerPoint or Google Slides, then print at actual size. Blank copies are included.

Download free on TPT

It opens on Teachers Pay Teachers, where the file lives. A free TPT account is needed to download.

Ways to use it

  1. Score one student from 1 to 3 on each expectation after every block of the day, and use the notes column to jot what happened.
  2. Run the on-task interval sheet during a short observation when a team or family meeting needs data instead of a general impression.
  3. Retype the three expectations so the sheet matches your own classroom rules before you print the blank copies.
