What is in the file

A free Thanksgiving week printable for grades 3 to 8, made to go with a free Thanksgiving week slide lesson. Students read the one surviving English eyewitness account of the 1621 harvest word for word and work out who wrote it, who is "we" and who are "they", build a timeline from long ago to 1863, mark four statements true or false, circle the specific thank yous, and write a specific thank you of their own. The Wampanoag perspective is part of it. You get two student pages, and a teacher page with the answer key and notes on page 4. It pairs with the free slide lesson, and the teacher page inside tells you where to find it.

Made for third grade through eighth grade. You get 5 pages, as an editable PowerPoint and a print-ready PDF.
