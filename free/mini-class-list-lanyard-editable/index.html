FREE Mini Class List Lanyard Editable

What it is

An editable mini class list sized for a badge pouch on your lanyard, plus a transportation card that shows who goes home how. Both include a black and white copy that saves ink.

Best for
Kindergarten through grade 3
Prep
Type your roster once in PowerPoint or Google Slides, print small at actual size, and slide it into a standard badge pouch.
Grades
Kindergarten, 1st grade, 2nd grade, 3rd grade

It opens on Teachers Pay Teachers, where the file lives. A free TPT account is needed to download.

Ways to use it

  1. Wear the roster on your lanyard for fire drills, lunch lines and dismissal, so you can check names without walking back to your desk.
  2. Fill in the transportation card at the start of the year and update it when plans change, so dismissal is never a guess.
  3. Hand a substitute a copy of the class list and the transportation card so they have names and dismissal details on hand.
