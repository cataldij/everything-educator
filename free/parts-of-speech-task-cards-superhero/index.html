Parts of Speech Task Cards FREE Superhero

What it is

36 printable superhero task cards where students decide whether a word is a noun, verb, adjective or adverb, with an answer key. Cards 1 to 12 are easy, 13 to 24 are medium and 25 to 36 are hard.

Best for
Grades 2 and 3
Prep
Print and cut apart the 36 cards, and keep the answer key handy for checking.
Grades
2nd Grade, 3rd Grade
Type
Activities

Download free on TPT

It opens on Teachers Pay Teachers, where the file lives. A free TPT account is needed to download.

Ways to use it

  1. Play SCOOT: put one card on each desk and have students rotate on your signal, writing each answer on their own paper.
  2. Set out the easy, medium and hard cards at three literacy stations so students move up as they get confident.
  3. Pull a few cards for morning work, a bell ringer or early finishers who need a quick grammar check.
