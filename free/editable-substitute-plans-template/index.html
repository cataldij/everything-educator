Free Editable Substitute Plans Template

What it is

An editable one-day substitute plan set in PowerPoint and PDF: a welcome letter, two Today's Plans pages, one built around an elementary day and one around secondary periods, plus an info page, emergency and dismissal pages, plus a how did today go form.

Best for
Kindergarten through grade 12
Prep
Edit the text boxes in PowerPoint or Google Slides, then print at actual size.

Download free on TPT

It opens on Teachers Pay Teachers, where the file lives. A free TPT account is needed to download.

Ways to use it

  1. Fill in the Today's Plans sheet the night before an absence so the sub sees each block or period in order.
  2. Complete the info and emergency and dismissal pages once and keep them in a folder or binder for the whole year.
  3. Leave the how did today go form on your desk so the sub can report back on the day.
