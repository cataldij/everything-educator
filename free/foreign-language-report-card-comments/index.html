Free Foreign Language Report Card Comments

What it is

A sampler of 50 report card comments for world language teachers, written in English for families, in three sections: openers and closers, interpersonal communication, and participation in the language. Each comment has a level tag and a code.

Best for
Grades 6 to 12, Spanish, French and other world languages

Ways to use it

  1. Choose one strength, one area to grow and one next step for each student, and jot down the comment codes before you open your report card system.
  2. Copy the comments you picked into your grading system and swap [Name] for the student's name.
  3. Use the level tags to pull Novice comments for Levels 1 and 2 and Intermediate comments for Levels 3, 4 and AP.
