What is in the file

Free foreign language report card comments for middle and high school Spanish, French and every world language: 50 ready-to-paste comments in 3 sections, tagged Novice, Intermediate or All levels, so they fit Levels 1 to 4 and AP. This sampler has warm openers and closers, interpersonal communication comments for conversation, and participation comments for effort, risk-taking and staying in the language. Every comment uses [Name] instead of he, she or they and is 150 characters or fewer, and you get an editable PowerPoint and a printable PDF. It is a sample of the full Foreign Language Report Card Comments bank, which adds 200 more comments in 14 more sections (explaining proficiency, interpretive listening, interpretive reading, presentational speaking, presentational writing, vocabulary and grammar, culture and comparisons, work habits, assessments and missing work, conduct and collaboration, special situations, Level 4 and AP, the end of the year and family support at home), editable Word files for Spanish, French or any other language, a three-move comment guide, a proficiency page in plain words, word swaps and comment planner pages for 30 students.
