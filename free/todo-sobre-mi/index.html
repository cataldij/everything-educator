Todo Sobre Mí GRATIS

What it is

Two editable all about me worksheets in Spanish: Todo Sobre Mí has six sentence starters about the student, and Sobre Mí has eight prompts on favorites and goals for the year. Each comes pre-filled as an example and blank.

Best for
Kindergarten through grade 3, Spanish-language classrooms
Prep
Print one copy per student, or edit the questions first.

Download free on TPT

It opens on Teachers Pay Teachers, where the file lives. A free TPT account is needed to download.

Ways to use it

  1. Hand out Todo Sobre Mí on the first day so students finish the six sentences and you learn who they are.
  2. Use Sobre Mí in the first week to collect favorite books and subjects, plus one thing each student wants to learn this year.
  3. Edit the prompts in PowerPoint before printing, or show the filled-in copy as a model when students need to see an example.
