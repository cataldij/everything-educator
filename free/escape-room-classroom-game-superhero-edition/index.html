HOW IT PLAYS

  1. Mastermind has trapped your class inside the supervillain lair.
  2. Split into 3 teams OR play as a whole class collaboration.

  4. Puzzle 1 CIPHER: 4 vocab words encoded, decode them to reveal a 4-digit code.
  5. Puzzle 2 SEQUENCE: 6 problems with numeric answers, order them for a sequence.
  6. Puzzle 3 LOGIC CODE: 4 word-problem clues each give one digit.
  7. Puzzle 4 FINAL LOCK: combine codes from 1+2+3 = the vault combination.
  8. Hints available, each costs 1 point penalty. Teacher controls hint reveals.
  9. Escape in 30-45 min wins! Stats slide tracks time + hints used.
