Using the resources

  • A free file downloaded here may be used by one teacher with that teacher's own students, including printing as many copies as those students need.
  • Please do not resell the files, post them on a public website or a shared drive outside your school, or claim them as your own work.

Accuracy

Resources are written carefully and used in a real classroom first, but they are offered as they are, with no warranty. You know your students and your standards better than I do, so check that a resource fits before you hand it out.
